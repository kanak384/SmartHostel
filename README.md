SmartHostel 🏠

AI-powered hostel management and student companion

Team ARISE | InnoHacks 4.0

SmartHostel is our idea for making hostel life a little simpler for students and easier to manage for wardens.

We noticed that many small hostel problems — like reporting a broken fan, finding hostel timings, checking notices, or knowing the status of a complaint — are usually handled through different places such as WhatsApp, verbal communication, notice boards, or registers.

So, we thought: why not put all of this in one place?

That's where SmartHostel comes in.

---

💡 What is SmartHostel?

SmartHostel is a centralized platform where students can access important hostel information and report problems, while wardens can manage and track those problems from one dashboard.

Our main idea is:

Report → AI Triage → Admin Assignment → Track → Resolve

For example, a student can report:

«“The fan in my room is not working.”»

They can also add a photo.

The system can then assist in identifying the issue as:

Electrical → Medium Priority

The warden can assign it to the appropriate maintenance staff, and the student can track its status until it is resolved.

---

❓ The Problem

Hostel management may seem simple, but there are many small problems that become difficult to track.

Some common issues are:

- Complaints getting lost in chats or verbal communication
- Hostel rules and notices being available in different places
- Students not knowing the status of their complaints
- Difficulty finding important facilities and their timings
- Wardens having to manually keep track of multiple complaints

We want to solve these everyday problems through one simple platform.

---

✨ Main Features

🛠️ Complaint Reporting

Students can report problems with a description and, if needed, a photo.

🤖 AI-Assisted Triage

AI can help suggest the category and priority of a complaint.

For example:

"Fan not working → Electrical → Medium Priority"

The AI is meant to assist the admin, not replace their decision.

📊 Admin Dashboard

Wardens can see reported issues in one place and keep track of what needs attention.

The dashboard can also show patterns such as which types of complaints occur most often.

🗺️ Hostel Map

Students can find important hostel locations such as blocks and facilities.

⏰ Facility Timings

Important timings such as mess, laundry, medical/help desk, and warden office timings can be maintained in the platform.

📢 Notices

Important hostel announcements can be kept in one place instead of being scattered across different communication channels.

📜 Rules & Essentials

Students can quickly access hostel rules, guidelines, and useful checklists.

🚨 Emergency Information

Important emergency/help information can be made easily accessible.

---

🔄 How It Works

A basic complaint goes through this process:

Student reports a problem
          ↓
Adds description / photo
          ↓
AI suggests category & priority
          ↓
Admin reviews the complaint
          ↓
Maintenance is assigned
          ↓
Student tracks the status
          ↓
Problem is resolved

The goal is to make the process more organized for everyone involved.

---

🧠 Where AI Fits In

AI is mainly used for complaint triage.

Instead of making the admin manually sort every complaint, the system can look at the complaint description and suggest:

- What type of problem it is
- How urgent it might be

For example:

"Water leaking from the bathroom tap"

        ↓ AI

Category: Plumbing
Priority: Medium

The admin can then review the suggestion and take the final decision.

🛠️ Technology We Plan to Use

For the MVP, our planned technology stack includes:

- Frontend: React
- UI: Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Firebase
- Database: Firestore
- File Storage: Firebase Storage
- Authentication: Firebase Authentication
- AI: Gemini / Google AI Studio

The exact implementation may be refined while building the MVP.

🎯 Our MVP

We don't want to try to build everything at once.

Our initial MVP will focus on the most important part of SmartHostel:

- Student complaint reporting
- Photo upload
- AI-assisted categorization
- Priority suggestion
- Admin dashboard
- Complaint assignment
- Status tracking

After that, features such as the hostel map, notifications, and AI hostel assistant can be developed further.
